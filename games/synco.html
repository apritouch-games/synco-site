Synco

Match minds. Guess pairs. Score together.

A fun mind-matching word game where one player creates word pairs and others try to guess them.

About the game

Synco is a mind-matching word game: one player creates noun + adjective pairs, and the others try to guess them.

  • Match mindsThink like the player who made the pairs.
  • Guess pairsFind noun + adjective matches before the round ends.
  • Score togetherEach guessed pair gives 1 point.

Game Rules

Synco is a mind-matching game. One player creates word pairs: noun + adjective. The others try to guess those pairs.

+1

Each guessed pair gives 1 point.

01

Circle

Players work together against the game.

  1. A player takes the phone and makes pairs.
  2. The other players do not look.
  3. Then the others try to guess their pairs.
  4. Each guessed pair adds points to the shared score.
  5. After the round, the turn passes to the next player.
  6. You win when the shared score reaches the goal.
Example

Player 1 made 5 pairs. The others guessed 3 pairs. Shared score: +3 points. Next turn — Player 2.

02

Teams

Teams compete against each other.

  1. The team chooses a player who will make pairs.
  2. The others do not look.
  3. Then players from the same team try to guess their pairs.
  4. The team gets points for guessed pairs.
  5. After the round, the turn passes to the next team.
  6. The first team to reach the target score wins.
Example

Team 1 made 5 pairs. Team 1 guessed 3 pairs. Team 1 gets +3 points. Next turn — Team 2.

Victory comes when the point goal is reached.
