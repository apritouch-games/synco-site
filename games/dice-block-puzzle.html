Dice, blocks and clever decisions

Dice Block Puzzle brings together the excitement of rolling dice and the strategy of solving block puzzles. Roll two dice, choose your block shape, and find the perfect spot on a 10×10 grid.

Easy to learn, challenging to master. Every roll creates a new opportunity — and every decision counts.

How to play

01

Roll Dice. Fit Shapes. Think.

  1. Roll two realistic 3D dice.
  2. Multiply the dice values to determine your block's area.
  3. Choose a rectangular shape that fits.
  4. Place your block strategically on the board.
  5. Plan your next moves and make every cell count!
02

Same area. Different shapes.

Roll 2 and 4 to get an area of 8 cells. Choose a 1×8, 8×1, 2×4 or 4×2 rectangle to fit the space on your board.
