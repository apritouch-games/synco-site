Xachbar

Solve Armenian crosswords. Discover new words.

Xachbar is a simple and fun Armenian crossword puzzle game with helpful hints and daily progress.

About the game

Solve Armenian crossword levels, discover new words and enjoy a calm challenge for your mind.

  • Discover wordsGrow your Armenian vocabulary as you solve each crossword.
  • Use helpful hintsGet a little help when a difficult word slows you down.
  • Keep your progressComplete levels, earn stars and follow your daily progress.

How to play

Choose a crossword, solve its Armenian words and continue through the map at your own pace.

★

Every completed crossword moves your progress forward.

01

Solve a crossword

Fill the grid one Armenian word at a time.

  1. Choose an available level on the map.
  2. Read the clues and inspect the crossword grid.
  3. Enter the Armenian words that match the clues.
  4. Use a hint if you need help with a difficult answer.
  5. Complete the grid and continue to the next challenge.
02

Build your progress

Return every day and keep discovering words.

  1. Earn stars by completing crossword levels.
  2. Open new challenges as you move through the map.
  3. Collect coins and spend them on helpful hints.
  4. Track your results and daily activity.
  5. Play at your own pace and improve your vocabulary.
