How to play

Use vocabulary, logic, memory and general knowledge to restore the quotation one answer at a time.

“”

The more words you solve, the more of the hidden quote is revealed.

01

Solve the clue words

Find the answer that matches each definition.

  1. Choose a category and open a puzzle.
  2. Read a clue and enter the correct word.
  3. Use a hint if you get stuck.
02

Reveal the quotation

Matching numbers connect answers with the hidden quote.

  1. Correct letters appear everywhere the same number is shown.
  2. Use the revealed pattern to solve the remaining words.
  3. Complete the level by restoring the full quotation.
